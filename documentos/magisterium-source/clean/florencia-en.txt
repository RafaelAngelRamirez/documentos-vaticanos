Sessio Laetentur caeli Let the heavens rejoice and the earth exult: for that long division of Easterners and Westerners has been removed from the universal Church. We define that the Holy Apostolic See and the Roman Pontiff have primacy throughout the world, and that the Roman Pontiff himself is the successor of the blessed Peter, prince of the apostles, and true vicar of Christ and head of the entire Church and father and doctor of all Christians; and that to him in the blessed Peter has been given by our Lord Jesus Christ the full power to feed, govern and govern the universal Church.
