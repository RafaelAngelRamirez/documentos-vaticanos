1
Canon 1 de electione Romani pontificis So that in the election of the Roman Pontiff the Church does not remain vacant for a long time, we establish that the Roman Pontiff be held without any retraction by the one in whom both parties of the cardinals have consented. If anyone, however, supported by a smaller number, has entered, let him be struck with anathema.

2
Canon 2 No one may be elected bishop except someone who has reached the age of thirty, and was born of legitimate marriage, and is literate and has proven customs. The election must be made by the canons or clergy, not by the laity.

3
Canon 3 of pluralitate beneficialum No one obtains at the same time several cathedral churches or several benefices that have a cure for souls, except by dispensation from the Apostolic See. Whoever otherwise has them, be content with one and resign the others.

4
Canon 4 Bishops and archbishops must not demand unjust exactions from clerics, neither for chrism, nor for dedication, nor for visitation, beyond moderate and ancient usage.

5
Canon 5 No cleric is promoted to orders for money. We order that the simoniacs be deposed and that the money be restored.

6
Canon 6 Jews and Saracens are not to be preferred to Christians in public offices, nor are Christians to be subject to them as lords in insult to the faith.

7
Canon 7 Those injured by leprosy are separated from the treatment of the healthy, and have their own churches and cemeteries, as far as possible, and tithes and offerings are provided for them.

8
Canon 8 Those who have beaten or stripped clerics, unless they have given satisfaction, are separated from communion, and appeal to the Apostolic See for absolution.

9
Canon 9 of haereticis We wound with anathema the Cathars and Patarines and other heretics, and their defenders and recipients, and we impose on the secular powers to extirpate them, saving the penance of those who convert.

10
Canon 10 of the Cross Signatis To all the faithful of Christ who have taken the sign of the cross for the liberation of the Eastern Church, and have personally worked there, we forgive the full remission of their sins, of which they have been truly contrite in heart and confessed by mouth.
