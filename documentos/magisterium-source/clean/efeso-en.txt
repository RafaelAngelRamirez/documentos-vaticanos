1
Canon 1 If any provincial metropolitan, separated from the holy and ecumenical synod, has transferred to the congregation of apostasy, or later transfers, or feels the things of Celestius: he cannot do anything at all against the provincial bishops, being in any way expelled from all ecclesiastical communion. But also the provincial bishops themselves and the surrounding metropolitans of the Orthodox faith will have the power to depose him.

2
Canon 2 If some provincial bishops depart from the holy and ecumenical synod and move into apostasy, or later move, or feel the things of Celestius, these too are in any way alien to the episcopate and fall from the degree of the priesthood.

3
Canon 3 If some clerics in each city or region have been deprived by Nestorius and those who are with him because of the right faith, we command that the righteous be restored by the holy synod. And we absolutely do not command that also the clerics who consent to the Orthodox and Ecumenical Synod in no way be subjugated by the Nestorians and their supporters.

4
Canon 4 If some clerics have departed and dare to feel in private or in public the things of Nestorius and Celestius: these too are deemed deposed by the holy synod.

5
Canon 5 If some, because of indecent acts, have been deprived of all ecclesiastical communion, whether episcopal or clerical, by the holy synod or by their own bishops, and these have attempted or will attempt in vain to receive restitution from Nestorius or those who are with him: we have deemed it right that they should remain unrestituted and should nevertheless be deposed.

6
Canon 6 Likewise, also if some want in any way to upset the things that were done about each one in the holy synod gathered at Ephesus: the holy synod has defined that, if they are bishops or clerics, they fall completely from their own grade; and if laymen, let them be anathematized.

7
Canon 7 Having read these things, then, the holy synod sanctioned that it is licit for no one to utter, write, or compose another faith outside of that defined by the holy Fathers who gathered in Nicaea of Bithynia with the Holy Spirit. Those who dare to compose, or utter, or offer another symbol of faith to those who want to convert to the knowledge of the truth, whether of gentility, now of Judaism, or of any heresy: these, if they are bishops or clerics, let the bishops be strangers to the episcopate and the clerics to the clergy; and if laymen, let them be anathematized. Likewise, if you are caught, whether bishops, clerics, or laymen, feeling or teaching the things contained in the libel expounded by the presbyter Charisius concerning the incarnation of the only begotten Son of God, or the clumsy and perverse dogmas of Nestorius: let them be subject to the sentence of this holy and ecumenical synod: so that the bishop is alien to the episcopate and deposed; the cleric also falls from the clergy; and the layman, as has been said, be anathematized.

8
Canon 8 The ancient thing, in force from the beginning according to the Nicene canons and ancient customs, may also be preserved in Egypt and in the most holy Churches therein, so that the bishop of Alexandria has power over all. And in the other dioceses and provinces the privileges proper to each one are preserved everywhere. If anyone, outside the present definition, causes annoyance and confuses the custom that has prevailed from the beginning and the canons: he, as it pleases the entire holy and ecumenical synod, will receive just retribution. Definition of the Nicene faith The holy and ecumenical synod, gathered in Ephesus, defines that it is not licit to utter, write or compose another faith outside of that which was defined by the holy Fathers gathered in Nicaea of ​​Bithynia. Those who dare to compose or utter or offer another symbol of faith to those who want to convert to the knowledge of the truth, whether of gentility, now of Judaism, or of any heresy: these, if they are bishops or clerics, let the bishops be strangers to the episcopate and the clerics to the clergy; and if laymen, let them be anathematized. Synodic Epistle to Nestorius We command that Nestorius, who dividedly confesses God the Word and man and refuses to call the holy Virgin Mother of God, be alien to the priestly degree and to all ecclesiastical communion, unless he repents and confesses the orthodox faith.
