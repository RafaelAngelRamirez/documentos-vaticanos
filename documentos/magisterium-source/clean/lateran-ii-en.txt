1
Canon 1 of simonia Those who have reached orders or dignities through simoniacal perversity are completely excluded from them. And those who from now on dare to order or order themselves simonically, both those who give and those who receive will be subject to deposition.

2
Canon 2 of clericis coniugatis It was established by the holy Fathers and is renewed by us: that no one in the subdiaconate or above take a wife, nor retain one already taken. Whoever acts otherwise falls from office and profit.

3
Canon 3 We completely prohibit priests, deacons, subdeacons and monks from having concubines or wives. We also judge that marriages contracted by such persons should be dissolved, and that the persons should be reduced to penance.

4
Canon 4 We deprive clerics constituted in sacred orders who have joined wives or public concubines of their office and ecclesiastical benefit, until they present worthy satisfaction.

5
Canon 5 of usuris We prohibit usury in any way. Those who are found to be lenders are separated from the communion, unless they repent of this plague and restore what was wrongly taken.

6
Canon 6 We remove from the thresholds of the Church the arsonists and their supporters, and, unless they repair the damage, we bind them with anathema.

7
Canon 7 Those who have taken up arms against Christians for earthly gain, unless, admonished by the prelates, they desist, are separated from communion.

8
Canon 8 Bishops do not ordain clerics of another diocese without resignation letters from the bishop himself. The ordering made otherwise is void.

9
Canon 9 Monks and canons regular do not leave monasteries without permission from the abbot, nor do monks' possessions be converted to secular uses.

10
Canon 10 of haeresibus We anathematize heresies and sects that deviate from the Catholic faith, and we impose on bishops to strive to extirpate them in their parishes.
