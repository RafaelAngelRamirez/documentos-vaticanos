1
Canon 1 The present council has been assembled for the honor of the Apostolic See and the benefit of the Roman Church, and for the universal reform of the ecclesiastical state.

2
Canon 2 de depositione Friderici We depose and deprive Frederick, who is appointed emperor of the Romans, for enormous excesses and persecution of the Church, with the authority of Almighty God and of the blessed apostles Peter and Paul and ours, from the empire and from all honor and dignity, absolving from the oath all those who are subject to him by an oath of fidelity, and more strictly inhibiting that no one from now on dare to obey him as emperor.

Canon 1 The present council has been assembled for the honor of the Apostolic See and the benefit of the Roman Church, and for the universal reform of the ecclesiastical state.

2

3
Canon 3 Those who knowingly receive the excommunicated and interdicted in communion, and participate with them in divine offices, may be wounded with the same censure, until they repent.

4
Canon 4 Prelates do not offend the churches with excessive procurements and exactions. The old moderation is observed.

5
Canon 5 of Terra Sancta For the relief of the Holy Land and against the enemies of the faith, tithes and subsidies of ecclesiastical revenues are faithfully collected, as the Apostolic See has decided to order, and destined for the intended use.

6
Canon 6 Clerics should not carry weapons except in case of necessity, nor should they meddle more than necessary in secular affairs.

7
Canon 7 The elections of bishops are made freely and canonically, without lay pressure. The election made by force is void.

8
Canon 8 We command that usury and illicit contracts by clerics and laymen be completely avoided. The stubborn be coerced with ecclesiastical censure.

9
Canon 9 We strike heretics and their supporters, unless they have returned to the unity of the faith, with anathema, and we judge that the secular arm should be helped.

10
Canon 10 We command that all this decreed by the present council be firmly observed by all the faithful of Christ.
